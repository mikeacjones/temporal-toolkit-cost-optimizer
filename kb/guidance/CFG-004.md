---
id: CFG-004
title: Provisioned Capacity above use
why_it_costs: Each TRU after the first commits a minimum number of Actions every hour, and Temporal Cloud bills up to that floor when the Namespace uses less.
fix: Lower the TRUs, or return to on-demand capacity, and raise them only around known spikes.
tradeoffs: Fewer TRUs means less guaranteed headroom. Keep them for planned events, and automate the change around them.

The Namespace has more than one TRU, and on most days its usage sits at or below the floor those TRUs commit. When usage is under the floor in an hour, Temporal Cloud records extra Actions up to it, so the TRUs set the bill rather than the Workflows.

tcopt reads daily usage, and the floor applies per hour, so a day can be under the floor in some hours and over it in others. Usage includes the floor's own Actions, so tcopt doesn't estimate how many Actions lowering the TRUs would save.
