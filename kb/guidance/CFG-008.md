---
id: CFG-008
title: Schedule storage
why_it_costs: Every Schedule runs an internal Workflow that stays open, so its history sits in Active Storage.
fix: Delete Schedules you no longer use, and replace a Schedule's whole spec when you update it.
tradeoffs: None for unused Schedules. Replacing the spec means sending the full set of times on each update.
docs:

snippets:

locator:

---

The Schedule is paused or has no upcoming runs, its internal Workflow history is large, or its spec has many entries. Every Schedule keeps an open internal Workflow, and open histories bill Active Storage for as long as they exist.

A spec with many entries usually means updates add times instead of replacing them, so the spec grows with each update.
