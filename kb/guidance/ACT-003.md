---
id: ACT-003
title: No retry limit on an Activity that retries
why_it_costs: With no maximum attempts and no schedule-to-close timeout, nothing stops an Activity from retrying for as long as its dependency is down. Each retry is one Action.
fix: Set MaximumAttempts, or a ScheduleToCloseTimeout that bounds how long the Activity may keep trying.
tradeoffs: A limit means the Activity can fail for good. The Workflow has to handle that failure, for example by compensating or by waiting for a person.

This Activity retried, and its Retry Policy has no limit on attempts or total time. That's the default, and it's often right for internal calls. For calls to third-party or paid APIs, a long outage turns into a long run of billed retries.

This finding is advisory: it reports a risk, not Actions spent today. Choose a limit that matches how long the call is worth retrying.
