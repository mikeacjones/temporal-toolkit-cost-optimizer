---
id: STO-002
title: Retention longer than needed
why_it_costs: Retained Storage bills each closed history for every day of retention.
fix: Shorten retention to the number of days people look at closed Workflows, and export histories you must keep longer.
tradeoffs: Closed Workflows leave the Temporal Cloud UI and Visibility sooner. Check how far back your team investigates before shortening it.
docs:

---

The Namespace keeps closed Workflows longer than the 30-day default and doesn't export them. Retained Storage grows with the number of retention days, so a 90-day retention holds three times the closed history of a 30-day one.

tcopt can't see when people read closed Workflows, so it compares retention with the default rather than with actual use. The estimate assumes 30 days.
