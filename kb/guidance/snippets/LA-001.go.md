Before: a flaky lookup runs as a Local Activity and keeps retrying inside the Workflow Task.

```go
ctx = workflow.WithLocalActivityOptions(ctx, workflow.LocalActivityOptions{
	StartToCloseTimeout: 5 * time.Second,
})
err := workflow.ExecuteLocalActivity(ctx, LookupPrice, sku).Get(ctx, &price)
```

After: a regular Activity retries on the server with its own backoff.

```go
ctx = workflow.WithActivityOptions(ctx, workflow.ActivityOptions{
	StartToCloseTimeout: 5 * time.Second,
	RetryPolicy:         &temporal.RetryPolicy{InitialInterval: 2 * time.Second, MaximumAttempts: 10},
})
err := workflow.ExecuteActivity(ctx, LookupPrice, sku).Get(ctx, &price)
```
