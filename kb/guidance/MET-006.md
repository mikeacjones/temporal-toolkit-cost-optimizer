---
id: MET-006
title: Daily Actions at least twice the 7-day average
why_it_costs: Temporal Cloud has no spend cap, so a runaway Workflow, a retry loop, or a re-enabled Schedule keeps spending Actions until someone notices.
fix: Find the Workflow Types and Action types behind the spike and check what changed, starting with non-production Namespaces and Schedules that were paused. Then alert on daily Actions above twice the 7-day average.
tradeoffs: Growth in real business also crosses the threshold. If the spike is steady growth, set a new baseline instead.

---

The latest day's billable Actions are at least twice the average of the seven days before it, for the Namespace or for a Workflow Type. The finding lists the Workflow Types with the largest increase.

Spikes like this often come from a bad deploy, a retry storm, or a staging Schedule turned back on by mistake. Compare the spike's start with deploy times, and check Schedules and non-production Namespaces first. Once the cause is fixed, keep an alert on the same rule so the next one is caught within a day.

The monthly figure assumes the spike continues at the latest day's level.
