title: A Timer used to debounce Signals
why_it_costs: The Workflow starts a Timer, 1 Action, after each Signal it receives.
fix: Buffer Signals in Workflow state and handle them in one place, or batch them before they reach Temporal.
tradeoffs: Debouncing helps when Signals arrive in bursts. If bursts are rare, the Timer costs an Action and saves nothing.

The Workflow starts a Timer in the same Workflow Task that handled a Signal, to wait for more Signals before acting. tcopt reports it when at least 80% of sampled runs show it.

If most Signals arrive alone, the wait adds an Action to every Signal without combining any. Keep incoming Signals in a slice and act on what's there each time the Workflow wakes. If the sender produces bursts, batch on the sender's side and send one Signal.

The saving counts each such Timer, 1 Action each.
