title: Timers from waits with a timeout
why_it_costs: A wait with a timeout starts a Timer, 1 Action, even when the condition is met first.
fix: Wait without a timeout where the code doesn't need one, and drain waiters before Continue-As-New.

The SDK starts a Timer for `AwaitWithTimeout`, a `condition` with a timeout, and a Selector that races a Timer. When the other side wins, the Timer is canceled, or, in the Go SDK by default, left pending until the run ends. Either way it cost an Action. tcopt flags runs that end with a canceled or still-pending Timer, and reports the pattern when at least half the sampled runs show it.

These waits often sit in Update or Signal handlers, where the handler doesn't need a timeout at all. Wait without one there.

The saving counts each such Timer, 1 Action each.
