title: Billable Actions well above what the sampled histories explain
why_it_costs: The Action metric shows more Actions of history-visible types than the sampled executions account for. Something the sample didn't catch, usually Activity retries, is spending Actions.
fix: Look at the Action types that make up the gap. Activity retries point to a retry storm (ACT-001) or Activities rejected by a Worker that doesn't register them (ACT-006); fix those first.
tradeoffs: None. This finding points at others; it has no saving of its own.

The metric reports Actions per Workflow Type and Action type. tcopt keeps only the Action types that Event History also records, then compares them with what the sampled histories estimate for the same Workflow Type. When the metric is at least 1.5 times higher, the sample is missing something.

The usual causes are retries that the sample happened to miss, or two Worker builds on one Task Queue where the older one rejects Activity types it doesn't register. The finding names the Action types behind the gap. Compare them with the Activity and retry findings for the same Workflow Type, or analyze a larger sample.

The two numbers cover different periods: the sample covers the analysis window, and the metric covers the minutes or days tcopt read. A burst of traffic in either can cause this finding on its own.
