---
id: CAN-001
title: Continue-As-New too often
why_it_costs: Each new run is a Workflow start, which is an Action. Runs that continue as new while their history is still small pay that Action far more often than the history limits require.
fix: Continue-As-New less often. Base the decision on history length or size, which the SDK reports, rather than on every iteration or a small count.
tradeoffs: Longer runs keep more history in Active Storage until they continue, and replay takes longer on a cold Worker. Stay well under the history limits.

Each matching run continued as new with fewer than 200 events. The estimate assumes runs could carry on to about 2,000 events, so only that share of today's starts would be needed. That is far below the 51,200-event limit.

Use the SDK's own signal where it has one: in Go, `workflow.GetInfo(ctx).GetContinueAsNewSuggested()` turns true as the history nears its limits.
