why_it_costs: Each Timer is 1 Action, however short it is.
fix: Remove Timers under a second, or replace them with the wait they stand in for.

The Workflow starts Timers shorter than a second. tcopt flags every Timer under 1 s.

A short sleep is often left over from code that yielded or paced a loop. A Workflow doesn't need to yield, and pacing calls to another service belongs in the Activity's retry policy or a rate limit on the Worker.

The saving counts each short Timer, 1 Action each.
