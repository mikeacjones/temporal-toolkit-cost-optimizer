---
id: ACT-005
title: Many tiny Activities in a row
why_it_costs: Each Activity is at least one Action. A chain of Activities that each take a few milliseconds, run one after another, costs one Action per step for work that could be one Activity.
fix: Where the steps share failure and retry needs, combine them into one regular Activity. Don't convert them to Local Activities.
tradeoffs: One Activity hides which step failed, retries all the steps together, and loses per-step visibility in the UI. Only merge steps that don't need those.

tcopt found five or more Activities that each ran alone in their own Workflow Task, each scheduled as soon as the previous one completed, with a median duration under 50 milliseconds. History can't show whether the Workflow branches between them, so this finding is advisory.

Merging them into one Activity removes one Action per merged step. Local Activities aren't the fix here: they retry together, give up Worker-level isolation, and keep their results in markers in history. Keep separate Activities where a step calls a different system or needs its own retries.
