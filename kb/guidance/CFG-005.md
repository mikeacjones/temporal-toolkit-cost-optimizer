---
id: CFG-005
title: Export with full retention
why_it_costs: Export bills one Action per exported Workflow, and Retained Storage still accrues for every retention day.
fix: Shorten the Namespace's retention, and read old histories from the export instead.
tradeoffs: Closed Workflows leave the Temporal Cloud UI and Visibility sooner. The export keeps the full history.
docs:
  - kb: cost/evaluate-cloud-actions#export

  - kb: cost/best-practices-cost-optimization#retention-periods
snippets:
  tcld: snippets/CFG-005.tcld.md
---

The Namespace exports closed Workflow histories and keeps them for its full retention period too. Export doesn't lower storage on its own: each closed history stays in Retained Storage until retention ends.

The estimate assumes 7 days of retention. Pick the number of days people actually look at closed Workflows in Temporal Cloud.
