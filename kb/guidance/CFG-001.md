---
id: CFG-001
title: Replication on a non-production Namespace
why_it_costs: A replica bills every Action and every byte of storage a second time.
fix: Remove the replica from development, test, and staging Namespaces.
tradeoffs: The Namespace loses failover to the replica's region. Non-production work rarely needs it, but check before removing it.
docs:
  - kb: cost/cloud-pricing#high-availability-feature-pricing
snippets:
  tcld: snippets/CFG-001.tcld.md
---

The Namespace has a replica, and its name or tags mark it as development, test, or staging. Temporal Cloud bills a replicated Namespace at twice its Actions and storage, so the replica doubles what the Namespace costs.

tcopt decides that a Namespace is non-production from words in its name or tags, such as `dev`, `test`, `qa`, or `staging`. It can't tell which Namespaces the team relies on, so confirm before removing a replica.
